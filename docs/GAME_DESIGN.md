# Game Design: Kaiju Keepers

## Pitch
Run your own kaiju zoo. Explore a big land, catch wild kaiju, find food to grow them
**giant**, steal from other zoos, and own the biggest kaiju on the server: the **Kaiju King**.

## Core loop
1. **Explore** biomes → find food (it regrows) and wild kaiju.
2. **Catch** kaiju (rarer = harder, may run away) or hatch eggs.
3. **Feed** them, favorite foods give 2x XP → they level up, grow bigger, earn more.
4. **Unlock** new biomes with cash + rebirths → new foods, new kaiju.
5. **Protect / steal**: food storage can be robbed; lock it, take it back, keep guard kaiju.
6. **Show off**: the biggest kaiju becomes the Kaiju King.

## Progression (why players stay for months)
| Time | Goal |
|---|---|
| Minutes | Find food, feed, catch commons |
| Hours | Unlock Beach & Snow, first Epic/Legendary, first mutation |
| Days | Rebirth 1–2 → Volcano, Crystal Caves, Kaiju King |
| Weeks | Rebirth 3 → Star Island, Mythic/Secret kaiju, full Index |
| Months | Every kaiju × mutation, top leaderboards, seasonal kaiju |

## Biomes
| Biome | Unlock | Foods | Kaiju |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌲 Forest | free | Meat, Golden Apple | Gloop, Rex, Kong, (Phoenix!) |
| 🏖️ Beach | $5K | Fish, Coconut | Pincher, Shellback, Kraken, (Hydra!) |
| ❄️ Snow Peaks | $75K | Ice Berry, Crystal Fruit | Frostbite, Tuskar, Rhinobug |
| 🌋 Volcano | $250K + Rebirth 1 | Fire Pepper | Drake, Phoenix, Hydra |
| 💎 Crystal Caves | $1.5M + Rebirth 2 | Crystal Fruit, Shadow Shroom | Rhinobug, Kraken, Voidmaw |
| ⭐ Star Island | $10M + Rebirth 3 | Star Food | Hydra, Phoenix, Voidmaw |
