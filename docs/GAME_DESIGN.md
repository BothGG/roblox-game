# Game Design: Kaiju Keepers

## Pitch
Collect baby kaiju, feed them until they're **giant**, steal food from other players,
and fight to own the biggest kaiju on the server: the **Kaiju King**.

## Core loop
1. **Get kaiju**: hatch eggs.
2. **Get food**: pick it up in the middle, buy it, or **steal it** from other bases.
3. **Feed**: kaiju level up, grow bigger and earn more money.
4. **Protect**: lock your storage, take food back from thieves, guard kaiju knock them away.
5. **Show off**: the biggest kaiju becomes the Kaiju King.

## Why players stay for months: layered goals
| Time | Goal |
|---|---|
| Minutes | Feed, steal, earn |
| Hours | New kaiju / rarity, mutations |
| Days | Max level, Kaiju King, Rebirths |
| Weeks | Complete the Index (every kaiju × mutation) |
| Months | Secret kaiju, top leaderboards, seasons |

## Done in v0.1
- 10 kaiju, 6 rarities, 5 traits (Growth, Income, Guard, Forage, Speed)
- 6 foods, 5 mutations with auras
- Stealing with carry / take back / guards / lock / join protection
- 3 eggs with hatch animation
- Kaiju King (crown, outline, +50% income)
- Rebirth (+income, +slots, +storage)
- Events: Meteor Feast, Blood Moon
- Saving, leaderboard, full effects system

## Monetization ideas (later)
- Game passes: 2x income, +storage, auto-collect, VIP lock (longer lock)
- Developer products: egg bundles, lucky potion (+mutation chance), instant growth
- Keep it fair: never sell "steal protection forever". Stealing is the fun.
